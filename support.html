Common questions

My tasks don't show up on my other devices

Sync uses iCloud. Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is turned on, and that there is free space in your iCloud storage. If tasks aren't syncing, Weeekly shows a notice at the top of the week explaining why.

I don't get reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Weeekly in Settings (System Settings › Notifications on the Mac). Reminders appear on each of your devices that has Weeekly installed.

How do I use Weeekly on all my devices?

Buy it once on the App Store, then download it on your Mac, iPad, iPhone and Apple Watch with the same Apple Account. There's nothing extra to pay.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete tasks in the App, or remove Weeekly's data from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage. We don't store your tasks anywhere else.
